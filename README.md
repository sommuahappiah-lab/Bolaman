The Bola Man 🗑️

Call. Collect. Clean.

The Bola Man is a Ghana-focused on-demand waste collection platform designed to connect households, businesses, waste collectors, and recycling partners.

🚛 What We Do

The Bola Man allows customers to request waste collection when they need it, instead of waiting for a collector to pass by.

Customers can eventually request pickups through:

- 📱 Mobile app
- 💬 WhatsApp
- ☎️ Phone
- 📟 USSD

👥 Who We Serve

- Households
- Shops and businesses
- Restaurants
- Offices
- Estates and communities
- Markets
- Events
- Construction sites

♻️ Our Vision

We want to move beyond simply collecting waste.

Our long-term vision is to create an organized waste-management ecosystem where waste can be:

Collected → Sorted → Recycled → Reused

This can create jobs for collectors while helping communities become cleaner and more organized.

🤝 Collectors

The Bola Man will work with existing waste collectors and tricycle operators by connecting them with customers who need collection services.

Collectors can receive jobs, accept pickups, complete collections, and build a record of their work.

💰 Business Model

Potential revenue streams include:

- One-time pickup fees
- Household subscriptions
- Business collection plans
- Estate/community contracts
- Event cleanup services
- Bulk waste collection
- Recycling and recovered-material sales

🚀 Current Stage

The Bola Man is currently in the prototype and development stage.

The initial goal is to test the service in one community, learn from real customers and collectors, and improve the system before expanding.

🇬🇭 Built for Ghana

The Bola Man is being designed with Ghanaian communities and the realities of local waste collection in mind.
